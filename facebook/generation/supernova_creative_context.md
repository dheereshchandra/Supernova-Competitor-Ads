## PART 1 — WHO SUPERNOVA IS (keep it short)
Supernova AI is an **AI spoken-English app for India**. A personal AI teacher, **Miss Nova**, listens to
you speak, corrects you live, and explains in *your* language — in ~10–15 minutes a day.

The customer (esp. the **2nd-payment** audience that matters most): **non-college, blue-collar / Tier 2–3
Indians** who understand some English but freeze when they have to speak. **Every ad targets Indians in
India.**

**Miss Nova** is the brand's recurring AI teacher: warm, patient, encouraging — a supportive friend /
older-sibling who happens to be a brilliant teacher. Never a scold, never a salesperson.

## PART 2 — THE PITCH POINTS (the benefit menu — land the ones the ad's beats invite)
Weave in the benefits the competitor's existing beats naturally invite — **usually 3 or more**, woven
**smoothly into the one continuous conversation**, never jammed in to hit a count. Smooth, natural flow
always wins; if a benefit doesn't fit a beat, use one that does. **Lead with the two flagship proof points
whenever they fit — "1 crore+ users" (social proof) and "improve in just 30 days" (a fast, concrete
outcome). These are our highest-converting claims and should rarely be missing.**

- **Personalization** — analyzes *your* level, finds where *you* struggle, builds a plan for *you*.
- **No-judgement / privacy** — make 1000 mistakes, no one watching or laughing, practise even at 3 a.m.
- **You SPEAK** (not type/read) — real *speaking practice*: you talk and it corrects you live. This is
  about *practising speaking*, never "speak loudly / out loud".
- **Not boring grammar lessons** — we don't drill grammar rules or textbook chapters; you learn by
  *speaking and being corrected in real time*. Use this contrast wherever a beat is about *how* you learn.
- **Explains in your language** (Hindi / Telugu / Tamil / Kannada / …) — the logic finally makes sense.
- **Mother-tongue reframe** — you learned your first language by *speaking* it; English works the same way.
- **Real-life practice** — the situations that actually scare people: a **job interview** (highest stakes),
  an **office / manager call**, the **bank**, the **airport**, a **government office**, everyday social
  talk. Name concrete, locally-relevant situations — not a generic "office calls".
- **Old methods failed** — 12 years of books and you still freeze when you have to speak.
- **Time is not the blocker** — when the ad raises "I have no time" / "English is hard", *answer it*: it is
  only **~10–15 minutes a day** (prefer the lower end, "just 10 minutes", when countering a no-time
  objection). Attach the time claim to the objection — never state it as a bare fact.
- **Practical proof** (use where they fit): **1 crore+ users** · **improve in just 30 days** ·
  **~7× cheaper than offline classes/coaching** (use the 7× comparative — never a rupee figure) · built by
  IITians · a level test · 1000+ practice scenarios.

**Affordability placement (the "7× cheaper" / cost claim) — DON'T force it in.** Bring the cost comparison
in ONLY when the conversation cues cost — a character asks the price, or worries "that might be expensive" /
"won't that cost a lot?" — then answer it with the 7×-cheaper line right there. If there is no cost cue
(e.g. a single-presenter monologue) but it still belongs, place it near the **END, right before the CTA** —
never jammed mid-script. No cue and no natural CTA-adjacent spot → just leave it out.

These are the **only** claims to make — do not invent features or outcomes. In particular, do NOT invent
your own result-timeline ("visible progress in a month", "fluent in a week") — **"30 days" is the one and
only time-to-result claim.**
